How To Play

Move: WASD / arrows   Look: mouse / drag   Interact: E / USE

Sprint: Shift   Crouch: C / Ctrl   Flashlight: F   Pause: Esc

Choose your role in single player. As seeker, wait in the lobby elevator and then tag every hider. As hider, use the head start to disappear before the AI seeker is released. There is no hunt timer.

Caught hiders can spectate. Use Q / E, [ / ], the arrow keys, or the on-screen buttons to switch between living players until the round ends.

The demons are nobody's allies. Every location has its own staff — The Bellhop and The Housekeeper keep the Grand Hotel; The Greeter, The Custodian and The Nightwatch still work Cinder Mall. They hunt hiders even during the hiding phase, and they hunt you. They remember a fleeing player's trail for a few seconds, including through the stairwell, so keep changing direction after breaking sight.

Flashlight charge is finite. A full light lasts about two minutes of continuous use. A caught player drops their flashlight; seekers and hiders can recover it, adding its remaining charge to their own up to 100%.

Sprinting is metered. Your STAMINA bar drains while you run and empties into a winded state you cannot sprint out of. It refills while you walk, faster standing still, and fastest crouched in cover.

Do not settle. Stay in one place and your SANITY meter climbs. Fill it and The Bellhop walks straight into the room you are hiding in. Changing rooms clears it — so does walking the halls, and the secret passages drain it.
