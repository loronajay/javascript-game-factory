    How to Play

    Three things. There is no fourth thing.

    1. 1

      Grab the ball

      Start the drag on the ball itself. Your aim always begins dead centre — a moving hoop never drags your reticle along with it.

    2. 2

      Pull back

      How far you pull is power, and nothing else. 80% is the shot that swishes; the meter is telling you the truth, not a difficulty curve.

    3. 3

      Angle, then release

      Pull off to one side and the ball goes the other way — and flattens out as it does. Straight back is the steepest, kindest arc. Against a moving rim, aim where it is going to be.
