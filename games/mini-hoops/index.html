    How to Play

    Nothing here is timed or scored. Shoot until it feels right.

    Grab the ball · pull back · release

    PRACTICE BALL
    SHOT POWER 0%
    • Length is power. How far you pull, and nothing else. The meter reports the real ratio — it is not hiding a forgiving window, and it is not marking the answer.
    • Angle is aim and arc. Pull off to one side and the ball goes the other way, flattening as it goes. Straight back is the steepest, kindest arc.
    • The reticle never chases the rim. Aim is always measured against where the hoop rests, so against a moving rim you lead it yourself.
