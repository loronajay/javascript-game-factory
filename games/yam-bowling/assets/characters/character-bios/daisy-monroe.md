# Daisy Monroe

**Age**  
23

**Hometown**  
Sacramento, California

**Occupation**  
Boutique sales associate

**Bowling Style**  
Balanced and instinctive. Daisy prefers simple lines, moderate hook, and making small adjustments rather than rebuilding her shot.

**Favorite Ball**  
Yam Red

**Personality**  
Friendly, competitive, upbeat, and much more stubborn than she initially appears.

**Bio**  
Daisy started bowling because her friends needed one more person for a league team and ended up becoming the only one who took it seriously. She rarely looks rattled and has an annoying habit of getting better the longer a match lasts. Off the lane, she is social, fashion-conscious, and usually the first person trying to organize the next night out.
