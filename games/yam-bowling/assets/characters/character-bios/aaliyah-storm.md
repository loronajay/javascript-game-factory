# Aaliyah Storm

**Age**  
25

**Hometown**  
Charlotte, North Carolina

**Occupation**  
Emergency medical technician

**Bowling Style**  
Direct power player with excellent composure during high-pressure frames.

**Favorite Ball**  
Midnight

**Personality**  
Focused, dependable, blunt, and unusually calm when things go wrong.

**Bio**  
Aaliyah has very little patience for panic. Whether she's working a shift or staring at a tenth-frame spare, her instinct is the same: assess what matters and handle it. Her bowling is powerful without being reckless, and she tends to become more dangerous as matches tighten. She isn't big on trash talk, but she does enjoy making people regret theirs.
