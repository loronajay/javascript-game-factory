# Zuri Banks

**Age**  
24

**Hometown**  
Oakland, California

**Occupation**  
Streetwear designer

**Bowling Style**  
Aggressive hook player with a taste for risky angles and dramatic pocket entries.

**Favorite Ball**  
Violet Arc

**Personality**  
Bold, creative, competitive, and unapologetically flashy.

**Bio**  
Zuri would rather lose attempting something memorable than win by playing scared. Her style on the lane mirrors her design work: sharp lines, strong choices, and no interest in blending into the background. She talks plenty during a match, but unlike most trash talkers, she usually has the confidence to throw immediately afterward.
