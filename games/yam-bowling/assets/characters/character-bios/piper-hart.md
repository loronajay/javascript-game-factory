# Piper Hart

**Age**  
24

**Hometown**  
Nashville, Tennessee

**Occupation**  
Singer and barista

**Bowling Style**  
Smooth finesse player with a soft release and patient hook.

**Favorite Ball**  
Pearl

**Personality**  
Warm, romantic, talkative, and deceptively competitive.

**Bio**  
Piper joined a bowling league mostly because it gave her somewhere to go after evening gigs. She quickly became attached to the ritual of it: the shoes, the music, the scorecards, and the familiar faces every week. Her gentle delivery doesn't look particularly threatening, which has resulted in more than one opponent realizing much too late that she hasn't left an open frame all night.
