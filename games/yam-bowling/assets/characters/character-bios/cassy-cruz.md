# Cassy Cruz

**Age**  
25

**Hometown**  
San Diego, California

**Occupation**  
Tattoo artist

**Bowling Style**  
Creative shot-maker who likes changing angles and experimenting with hook.

**Favorite Ball**  
Violet Arc

**Personality**  
Easygoing, artistic, mischievous, and highly competitive once challenged.

**Bio**  
Cassy sees lane geometry the same way she sees a blank sketch: there is usually more than one way to make the final image work. She enjoys strange spare attempts and occasionally chooses the difficult line simply because it looks more interesting. Most of the time she is relaxed enough to bowl with anyone, but telling her she “got lucky” is a reliable way to turn the next frame into a grudge match.
