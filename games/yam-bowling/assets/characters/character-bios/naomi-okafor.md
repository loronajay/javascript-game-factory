# Naomi Okafor

**Age**  
29

**Hometown**  
Dallas, Texas

**Occupation**  
Corporate attorney

**Bowling Style**  
Strategic all-rounder who changes equipment and angles readily instead of forcing one preferred line.

**Favorite Ball**  
Yam Red

**Personality**  
Sharp, confident, competitive, diplomatic, and extremely difficult to read.

**Bio**  
Naomi enjoys figuring people out almost as much as she enjoys figuring out lane conditions. She rarely rushes a decision and has a talent for making opponents feel like she's already anticipated whatever they're about to try. Her game isn't built around one signature trick; her strength is having an answer when the obvious plan stops working.
