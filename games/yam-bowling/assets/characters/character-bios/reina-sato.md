# Reina Sato

**Age**  
26

**Hometown**  
Los Angeles, California

**Occupation**  
Automotive marketing specialist

**Bowling Style**  
Technical and composed, favoring carefully shaped hook and precise pocket placement.

**Favorite Ball**  
Yam Red

**Personality**  
Poised, ambitious, stylish, and intensely competitive beneath a controlled exterior.

**Bio**  
Reina hates looking unprepared. She studies her line, commits to her adjustments, and expects herself to execute when it matters. Her polished image has made her one of the league's most recognizable bowlers, but she is far more interested in being respected for her game than merely noticed for her appearance. Losing rarely produces a scene; it usually produces several extra hours of practice.
