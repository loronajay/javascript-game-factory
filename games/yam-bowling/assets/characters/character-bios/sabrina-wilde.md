# Sabrina Wilde

**Age**  
27

**Hometown**  
New Orleans, Louisiana

**Occupation**  
Makeup artist

**Bowling Style**  
Unpredictable and expressive, switching between straight attacks and strong hook depending on mood and lane read.

**Favorite Ball**  
Hot Yam

**Personality**  
Dramatic, funny, fearless, and proudly inconsistent in everything except confidence.

**Bio**  
Sabrina considers boring bowling a greater offense than bad bowling. She likes loud reactions, ambitious shots, and making every league night feel like an event. Beneath the theatrics, however, she understands the game far better than she lets on. The jokes tend to disappear quickly when somebody gives her a reason to take the score seriously.
