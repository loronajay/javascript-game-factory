# Marisol Cruz

**Age**  
25

**Hometown**  
San Antonio, Texas

**Occupation**  
Elementary school physical education teacher

**Bowling Style**  
Smooth and repeatable with a gentle hook, steady tempo, and a strong spare game built on patient fundamentals.

**Favorite Ball**  
Pearl

**Personality**  
Warm, encouraging, organized, and quietly delighted by proving people wrong.

**Bio**  
Marisol is the person teammates ask to settle them down after two ugly frames. She explains adjustments simply, celebrates other people's good shots sincerely, and almost never lets frustration change her tempo. That friendliness sometimes causes opponents to underestimate her until they notice she has filled every frame and is smiling at the scoreboard.
