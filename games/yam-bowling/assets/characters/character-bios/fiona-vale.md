# Fiona Vale

**Age**  
29

**Hometown**  
Chicago, Illinois

**Occupation**  
Event coordinator

**Bowling Style**  
Adaptable all-rounder with strong spare discipline and excellent match management.

**Favorite Ball**  
Yam Red

**Personality**  
Organized, sociable, composed, and quietly controlling.

**Bio**  
Fiona is the person who somehow already has the reservation, the backup reservation, and everyone's shoe sizes before anyone else has decided where they're going. That same preparation carries onto the lane. She isn't committed to one flashy signature shot; she simply wants the correct shot for the situation and gets visibly annoyed when the situation refuses to cooperate.
