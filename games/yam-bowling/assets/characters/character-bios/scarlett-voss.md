# Scarlett Voss

**Age**  
31

**Hometown**  
New York City, New York

**Occupation**  
Luxury real-estate agent

**Bowling Style**  
Confident power-control game built around hard pocket hits and minimal wasted movement.

**Favorite Ball**  
Midnight

**Personality**  
Sophisticated, intimidating, competitive, and completely uninterested in false modesty.

**Bio**  
Scarlett expects to win and sees little reason to pretend otherwise. Her game is efficient, strong, and almost annoyingly composed, particularly when the other player starts unraveling. She enjoys expensive restaurants, immaculate presentation, and beating someone immediately after they announce how seriously they take bowling.
