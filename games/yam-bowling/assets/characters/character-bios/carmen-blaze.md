# Carmen Blaze

**Age**  
28

**Hometown**  
Phoenix, Arizona

**Occupation**  
Personal trainer

**Bowling Style**  
High-energy power bowling with an aggressive release and little fear of overcommitting.

**Favorite Ball**  
Hot Yam

**Personality**  
Fearless, charismatic, intense, and fully aware when all eyes are on her.

**Bio**  
Carmen does not believe in subtle entrances, subtle outfits, or subtle strikes. She attacks the lane with the same attitude she brings to training: commit completely or don't bother. Her confidence can cross into arrogance when she's winning, but she's equally willing to laugh at herself when an ambitious shot goes straight into the gutter.
