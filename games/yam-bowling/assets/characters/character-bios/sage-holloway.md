# Sage Holloway

**Age**  
28

**Hometown**  
Asheville, North Carolina

**Occupation**  
Florist and small-business owner

**Bowling Style**  
Patient control player who prioritizes clean releases and reliable spare shooting.

**Favorite Ball**  
Pearl

**Personality**  
Gentle, perceptive, patient, and more competitive than her demeanor suggests.

**Bio**  
Sage has the unnerving ability to remain pleasant while quietly destroying someone's score. She prefers smooth, repeatable bowling and rarely changes something unless she has a reason. Friends describe her as calming to be around; opponents usually stop using that description somewhere around the eighth frame.
