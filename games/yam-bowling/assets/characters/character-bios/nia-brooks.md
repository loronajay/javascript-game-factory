# Nia Brooks

**Age**  
25

**Hometown**  
Atlanta, Georgia

**Occupation**  
Dance instructor

**Bowling Style**  
Rhythm-based and smooth. Nia relies on a consistent approach and clean release rather than brute force.

**Favorite Ball**  
Pearl

**Personality**  
Confident, expressive, playful, and difficult to embarrass.

**Bio**  
Nia treats bowling almost like choreography: establish a rhythm, trust the motion, and don't interrupt it by overthinking. Her relaxed attitude sometimes makes opponents assume she isn't taking the match seriously until the strikes start stacking up. She loves crowded lanes, loud music, and any match with enough personality to become a story later.
