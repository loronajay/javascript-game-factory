# Skye Bennett

**Age**  
21

**Hometown**  
Denver, Colorado

**Occupation**  
College student and climbing-gym employee

**Bowling Style**  
Quick and athletic with strong speed control and an aggressive approach.

**Favorite Ball**  
Blue Bolt

**Personality**  
Competitive, impulsive, adventurous, and terrible at backing down from a challenge.

**Bio**  
Skye's hobbies tend to begin with somebody saying, “I bet you can't.” Bowling was no different. What started as a casual challenge became a regular obsession once she realized there was a measurable score attached to it. She likes fast games, difficult opponents, and anything that gives her an excuse to declare a rematch immediately afterward.
