# Echo Sterling

**Age**  
27

**Hometown**  
Brooklyn, New York

**Occupation**  
Music producer

**Bowling Style**  
Timing-heavy and rhythmic, using deliberate tempo changes to keep her release consistent.

**Favorite Ball**  
Hot Yam

**Personality**  
Cool, creative, confident, nocturnal, and selectively social.

**Bio**  
Echo hears rhythm in practically everything, including an approach to the foul line. She became serious about bowling after realizing how much good timing matters, and now treats each throw like a sequence that has to land on the right beat. She isn't especially talkative during matches, but a strike usually earns a small satisfied smile that somehow feels more disrespectful than yelling.
