# Hazel Ward

**Age**  
26

**Hometown**  
Minneapolis, Minnesota

**Occupation**  
Veterinary technician

**Bowling Style**  
Steady and practical, with an emphasis on spare conversion and avoiding unnecessary risk.

**Favorite Ball**  
Golden Spare

**Personality**  
Kind, dependable, understated, and stubborn when she decides something matters.

**Bio**  
Hazel doesn't care whether a shot looks impressive as long as the pins end up down. She built her game around consistency after getting tired of watching friends throw huge hooks and leave avoidable splits. She is friendly with almost everyone in the league, though her definition of friendship apparently includes refusing to give away a single frame.
