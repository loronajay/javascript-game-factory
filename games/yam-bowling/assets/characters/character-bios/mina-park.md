# Mina Park

**Age**  
23

**Hometown**  
Seattle, Washington

**Occupation**  
Game UI designer

**Bowling Style**  
Technical finesse with careful aim, controlled speed, and an obsession with repeatable timing.

**Favorite Ball**  
Green Machine

**Personality**  
Detail-oriented, competitive, witty, and prone to overanalyzing tiny mistakes.

**Bio**  
Mina started tracking her bowling sessions in spreadsheets as a joke and then kept doing it after the joke stopped being funny. She loves measurable improvement and can tell you exactly which part of a shot felt wrong before the ball reaches the pins. Her biggest opponent is often herself; a perfectly good game can still annoy her if she knows three frames could have been cleaner.
