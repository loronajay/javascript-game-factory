# Tessa Quinn

**Age**  
28

**Hometown**  
Boston, Massachusetts

**Occupation**  
Paralegal

**Bowling Style**  
Methodical spare specialist. Tessa values predictable angles and clean conversions over flashy shots.

**Favorite Ball**  
Golden Spare

**Personality**  
Dry, observant, disciplined, and relentlessly sarcastic.

**Bio**  
Tessa keeps mental notes on everything: lane tendencies, opponent habits, missed opportunities, and especially bad excuses. Her game is built around minimizing mistakes, which makes her particularly irritating to players waiting for her to collapse under pressure. She claims bowling is merely a hobby, although nobody with multiple pairs of bowling shoes believes her.
