# Imani Cole

**Age**  
30

**Hometown**  
Washington, D.C.

**Occupation**  
Architect

**Bowling Style**  
Geometric and deliberate. Imani builds shots around entry angle, consistency, and repeatable positioning.

**Favorite Ball**  
Green Machine

**Personality**  
Analytical, elegant, independent, and extremely patient.

**Bio**  
Imani genuinely enjoys the geometry of bowling. Where other players see a lane, she sees angles, tolerances, and a problem that can be solved. She is rarely the loudest competitor in the building, but her ability to make calm adjustments after a bad frame makes her difficult to shake. She has also been known to correct people's explanations of why they missed.
