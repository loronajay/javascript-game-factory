# Talia Dodson

**Age**  
24

**Hometown**  
Milwaukee, Wisconsin

**Occupation**  
Community recreation coordinator

**Bowling Style**  
Compact and controlled, prioritizing repeatable balance, readable ball motion, and relentless pocket pressure.

**Favorite Ball**  
Green Machine

**Personality**  
Grounded, competitive, loyal, energetic, and always ready to turn practice into a team challenge.

**Bio**  
Talia grew up treating the neighborhood lanes like an extension of the rec center and still knows how to make a group of strangers feel like a team. She loves drills, scorekeeping, and any challenge with rules clear enough to argue about. Her consistency is the product of stubborn repetition, but she makes it look like momentum everyone else should have joined sooner.
