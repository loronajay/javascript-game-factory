24

**Occupation**

**Bowling Style**

**Favorite Ball**

**Personality**  
Competitive, direct, mechanically minded, and allergic to unnecessary drama.

**Bio**  
Roxy likes machines because they usually tell you exactly why something went wrong. Bowling appealed to her for similar reasons: release it badly and the lane will expose you immediately. She throws hard, adjusts quickly, and has little sympathy for players who blame the equipment after every miss. Her own bad shots receive much harsher criticism.
