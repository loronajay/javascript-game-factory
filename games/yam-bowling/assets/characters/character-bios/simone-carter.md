# Simone Carter

**Age**  
30

**Hometown**  
Atlanta, Georgia

**Occupation**  
Event producer

**Bowling Style**  
Polished and adaptable, pairing a controlled benchmark line with confident spare shooting when the pressure rises.

**Favorite Ball**  
Golden Spare

**Personality**  
Composed, generous, stylish, decisive, and accustomed to taking charge when plans go sideways.

**Bio**  
Simone spends her workdays making complicated events look effortless, and she brings the same calm authority to league night. She notices details, remembers everyone's preferred lineup, and can reorganize a team without making anyone feel managed. On the lane she values clean execution over spectacle, although her celebrations become considerably less restrained after a difficult tenth-frame conversion.
