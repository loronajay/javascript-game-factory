# Claire Rowan

**Age**  
26

**Hometown**  
Portland, Oregon

**Occupation**  
Bookstore manager

**Bowling Style**  
Precision-first with conservative hook and excellent lane control.

**Favorite Ball**  
Green Machine

**Personality**  
Reserved, thoughtful, clever, and unexpectedly ruthless in close games.

**Bio**  
Claire is easy to overlook until the scorecard starts filling with clean frames. She enjoys the analytical side of bowling and is perfectly content winning without producing a single dramatic moment. Away from competition she is quiet and approachable, but put a one-pin match in front of her and the polite bookstore manager disappears surprisingly quickly.
