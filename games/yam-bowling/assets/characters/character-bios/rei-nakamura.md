# Rei Nakamura

**Age**  
28

**Hometown**  
Seattle, Washington

**Occupation**  
Cybersecurity analyst

**Bowling Style**  
Direct and data-driven, using firm speed, small angle changes, and a nearly straight power line when the lane gets unpredictable.

**Favorite Ball**  
Midnight

**Personality**  
Observant, dryly funny, private, disciplined, and allergic to unnecessary drama.

**Bio**  
Rei tracks patterns automatically: ball reaction, spare tendencies, who rushes after a miss, and which lane machine is overdue for maintenance. She keeps detailed notes but refuses to call them a system, mostly because she enjoys watching people guess. Her game is clean, efficient, and built to keep working after flashier plans begin to break down.
