**Bowling Style**

**Bio**  
Amara became fascinated with bowling mechanics after realizing how much a tiny change in posture could alter an entire shot. She approaches competition with the same patience she brings to her work: diagnose the problem, correct it, and move on. She rarely celebrates early and almost never reacts to trash talk, which tends to make the person talking even more uncomfortable.
