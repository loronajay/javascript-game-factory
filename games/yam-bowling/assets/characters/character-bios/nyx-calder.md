# Nyx Calder

**Age**  
27

**Hometown**  
Las Vegas, Nevada

**Occupation**  
Casino bartender

**Bowling Style**  
Heavy late hook with calculated aggression and a preference for dramatic pocket hits.

**Favorite Ball**  
Violet Arc

**Personality**  
Cool, teasing, mysterious, and extremely comfortable under pressure.

**Bio**  
Nyx works nights, sleeps strange hours, and has played an unreasonable amount of bowling after midnight. She enjoys close matches far more than easy ones and seems most comfortable when everyone else is tense. Opponents often struggle to tell whether she's concentrating, bored, or deliberately trying to make them nervous. Usually it's the third one.
