# Kevya Desai

**Age**  
27

**Hometown**  
Edison, New Jersey

**Occupation**  
Wedding choreographer

**Bowling Style**  
Rhythmic and high-rev, using quick footwork and a sharp late hook that looks looser than it actually is.

**Favorite Ball**  
Violet Arc

**Personality**  
Expressive, playful, perceptive, and fiercely serious once competition starts.

**Bio**  
Kevya can find the beat in almost anything, including a noisy approach and a badly timed ball return. Her work has made her excellent at reading nervous people, so she usually knows when an opponent is pressing before they do. She jokes through warmups, dances after strikes, and becomes unnervingly precise whenever a match gets close.
