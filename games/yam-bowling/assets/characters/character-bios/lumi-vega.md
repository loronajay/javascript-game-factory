# Lumi Vega

**Age**  
22

**Hometown**  
Miami, Florida

**Occupation**  
Nightclub photographer

**Bowling Style**  
Fast, reactive, and fearless. Lumi likes speed and commits to adjustments immediately.

**Favorite Ball**  
Blue Bolt

**Personality**  
Energetic, spontaneous, flirtatious, and almost impossible to keep still.

**Bio**  
Lumi discovered league bowling through a late-night staff outing and immediately decided the sport needed more noise, brighter clothes, and better victory pictures. She bowls quickly and hates standing around second-guessing herself. Her camera roll contains thousands of nightlife photos and an unreasonable number of slow-motion videos of her own strikes.
