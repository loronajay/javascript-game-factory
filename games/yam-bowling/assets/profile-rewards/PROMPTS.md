# Profile reward generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as polished 2.5D enamel or
metal sports crests for a competitive arcade-bowling UI. Every crest was framed
as one centered, text-free collectible with a bold silhouette that reads at 48
pixels, generous padding, crisp edges, no watermark, and no cast shadow. Exact
reward names remain live UI text.

Shared isolation prompt: use a perfectly uniform `#00ff00` chroma-key background
with no gradient, floor, glow, texture, or reflection; do not use green in the
subject. Outputs that arrived with native alpha were retained directly. Keyed
outputs were locally converted to alpha, centered, and optimized to 512px WebP.

- `pin-chaser.webp`: a Yam-red ball pursuing a glossy white pin across speed
  streaks and a target-ring shield; warm gold and gunmetal trim.
- `laser-focus.webp`: a single white pin held inside a cyan precision sight with
  one restrained red focal point; navy enamel and brushed silver circular rim.
- `precision-bowler.webp`: a ball following an exact amber line into the pocket,
  surrounded by lane geometry, mechanical calipers, titanium, and deep teal.
- `lane-legend.webp`: a luminous crystal lane passing through a monumental arch
  made from standing pins, with violet obsidian, moonlit silver, and antique gold.
- `perfect-game.webp`: a flawless pin-rack medallion crowned by a radiant gold
  ball, strike stars, black diamond facets, pearl enamel, and mirror gold.
- `split-decision.webp`: two isolated corner pins on fractured interlocking badge
  halves with a blazing ball threading a forked violet-and-magenta path.
- `comeback-kid.webp`: a charcoal bowling ball rising as a phoenix whose wings
  are lane arrows and ember trails, with one upright pin above it.
- `yam-champion.webp`: the ultimate tournament crest—an engraved gold crown with
  bowling-pin points above a championship lane, jewel ball, laurels, ruby enamel,
  amethyst accents, and an obsidian foundation.
