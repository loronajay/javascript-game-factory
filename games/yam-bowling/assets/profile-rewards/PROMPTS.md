# Profile reward generation prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool as polished 2.5D enamel or
metal sports crests for a competitive arcade-bowling UI. Every crest was framed
as one centered, text-free collectible with a bold silhouette that reads at 48
pixels, generous padding, crisp edges, no watermark, and no cast shadow. Exact
reward names remain live UI text.

The first collection used a uniform `#00ff00` isolation prompt when native alpha
was unavailable. The second requested transparent alpha directly. Outputs were
cropped to their alpha bounds, centered with consistent clear space, and
optimized to 384px WebP—well above the 46–52px cabinet display size.

- `pin-chaser.webp`: a Yam-red ball pursuing a glossy white pin across speed
  streaks and a target-ring shield; warm gold and gunmetal trim.
- `laser-focus.webp`: a single white pin held inside a cyan precision sight with
  one restrained red focal point; navy enamel and brushed silver circular rim.
- `precision-bowler.webp`: a ball following an exact amber line into the pocket,
  surrounded by lane geometry, mechanical calipers, titanium, and deep teal.
- `lane-legend.webp`: a luminous crystal lane passing through a monumental arch
  made from standing pins, with violet obsidian, moonlit silver, and antique gold.
- `perfect-game.webp`: a flawless pin-rack medallion crowned by a radiant gold
  ball, strike stars, black diamond facets, pearl enamel, and mirror gold.
- `split-decision.webp`: two isolated corner pins on fractured interlocking badge
  halves with a blazing ball threading a forked violet-and-magenta path.
- `comeback-kid.webp`: a charcoal bowling ball rising as a phoenix whose wings
  are lane arrows and ember trails, with one upright pin above it.
- `yam-champion.webp`: the ultimate tournament crest—an engraved gold crown with
  bowling-pin points above a championship lane, jewel ball, laurels, ruby enamel,
  amethyst accents, and an obsidian foundation.

## Second collection prompts

Every prompt requested one production-ready, text-free arcade-bowling UI crest,
strong readability at 40px, premium enamel-and-metal rendering, generous margin,
and genuine transparent alpha with no floor, backdrop, external border, or cast
shadow.

- `ice-in-the-tenth.webp`: a blue ice bowling ball shattering a red pressure
  ring beside a white pin, inside a sharp diamond-like cold-metal title crest.
- `spare-architect.webp`: a cobalt blueprint crest with a drafting compass, two
  pins, exact lane geometry, and restrained brass engineering details.
- `bracket-breaker.webp`: a cobalt ball physically smashing through a steel and
  orange tournament bracket, with flying fragments and a bold angular silhouette.
- `undisputed.webp`: a black-platinum championship belt seal centered on a white
  faceted bowling ball and surrounded by four distinct major-event jewels.
- `clean-card.webp`: an immaculate emerald-and-gold regulation scorecard with
  exactly ten cells, a decisive completion check, and no printed characters.
- `turkey-club.webp`: exactly three linked copper impact chevrons, three small
  falling-pin bursts, and one petrol-teal ball; explicitly no literal turkey.
- `road-tested.webp`: a distressed oxblood travel-case shield with venue panels,
  brass hardware, map markers, one pin, and a winding lane-road motif.
- `deep-bench.webp`: five visibly distinct jewel-colored bowling balls seated on
  a stepped champion bench, framed by platinum laurels and five spotlights.
