Roll it your way

01

Build a line

Use A / D to strafe and ← / → to aim, or use the sliders. The blue dot marks where the ball leaves the oil and begins to hook.

02

Time the spin

Tap Throw or Space to start the spin needle. Press again when it reaches the hook you want.

03

Commit the power

Each ball aims and charges at its own pace. Release in the gold window—holding too long drains power. More speed skids farther.
