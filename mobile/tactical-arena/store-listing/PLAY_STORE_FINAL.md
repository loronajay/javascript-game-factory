## Full description

```text
Tactical Arena is a turn-based strategy game built around small squads and meaningful decisions. Draft four fighters from a roster of 30, read the battlefield, and turn positioning, range, elevation, armor, magic, weather, and timing into a win.

Build Your Squad

Every fighter has distinct stats, reach, and signature ARTS. Pair durable front-line units with ranged specialists, support allies, summoners, or high-risk damage dealers. Your squad is only four fighters, so every choice matters.

Master the Battlefield

Positioning and range decide who controls the board. Physical damage is reduced by armor, magic cuts through it, and true damage ignores it. Weather can change the fight, while wounded units enter RAGE and become more dangerous.

Challenge Other Players

- Classic 1v1 and Draft 1v1
- Ranked duels with an ELO ladder
- Four-player free-for-all
- 2v2 team battles
- Quick Match and private room codes
- Friends, player profiles, records, badges, and rematches

Conquer the Campaign

Fight through 22 missions with objectives, star ratings, dialogue, unlockable units, and multi-stage boss encounters. Each mission changes the rules, the opposition, or the battlefield.

Practice Your Way

Five guided tutorials cover movement, combat, ARTS, and status effects. Battle the CPU on Easy, Normal, or Hard, then try Tempo Battle, where readiness gauges replace traditional turns.

Make It Yours

Collect hundreds of unit skins, unlock board themes, give units nicknames, earn Valor through play, and customize your profile.

Play without ads or energy timers. An internet connection and account are required for online multiplayer and social features.
```
