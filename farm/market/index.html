EVERYONE'S SQUARE

Market day.

The road from the farm ends in a walled square. Marigold at the Produce Merchant buys your harvest for tickets, and the Order Board pays better — plus Farming XP — for what the town needs today. The other stalls open as the farm grows. Whoever else is here, you'll see them.
