EVERYONE'S SQUARE

Market day.

The road from the farm ends in a walled square. Marigold at the Produce Merchant buys your harvest for tickets, and the Order Board pays better — plus Farming XP — for what the town needs today. The other stalls open as the farm grows. Whoever else is here, you'll see them.

        LIVESTOCK DEALER · HOLLISBuy young stock

        Every animal is one of a kind: its Yield, Quality, Growth and Hardiness are its own, and its grade comes from them. Each goes straight home to the first stall, barn floor or pen with room — move it on the farm with L.

          BUTCHER · OTTOSend livestock for meat

          Otto takes a grown animal and cuts it into meat for your harvest basket. Yield and age decide how many cuts — the most at its prime, twice its grown age — and its Quality, less the time it spent hungry, decides the grade. An animal sent here leaves your farm for good.

            SEED MERCHANT · JUNIPERBuy seeds

            Every seed your farm's shop sells, at the same price — and three on special each day for a quarter less, only here. Seeds go straight to your farm's Inventory.

              THE KITCHEN · BASILSell your cooking

              Basil sells permanent recipe cards for dishes outside the Cooking level tree. He also buys cooked dishes: a quarter above their raw ingredients for a plain plate, up to nearly three quarters more for three stars.

                  THE SAWMILL · BRAMSaw logs into planks

                  Every log saws into three planks of its own wood, for a ticket a log. A Sawmill of your own (Build mode · Props) saws for nothing.

                    THE SAWMILL · BRAMSell your furniture

                    Bram pays twice what a piece's planks are worth for a rough one, over three times for a varnished one. Only pieces on your shelf can be sold — anything standing on your farm stays there.

                            ORDER BOARD · Today's orders

                            Everyone in the square reads the same notices. Each pays once per farmer, better than the counters do, and every delivery earns XP in the skill it asks of: Farming for produce, Cooking for dishes, Fishing for fish, Husbandry for milk and wool.
