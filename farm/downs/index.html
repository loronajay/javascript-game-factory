        THE OVALRace board

        Post a race and whoever is here can enter. Friendly races are free; with a stake, every rider puts it up and the winner takes the pot, less a 10% house fee. The race runs on the server, so nobody's clock decides it.

          THE OVALBetting booth

          Back a rider in a race that is still taking bets. Every bet goes in one pool; when the race is run, the pool less a 10% house fee is shared by everyone who backed the winner, by how much they put in. Riders in a race cannot bet on it.
