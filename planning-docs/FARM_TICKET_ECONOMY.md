# Farm Ticket Economy v1

## Implemented product rules

- The Farm uses the same account-wide ticket wallet and generic permanent-item
  purchase endpoint as the Arcade Room.
- Starter ground/decor remains permanently available: Meadow, Post & Rail,
  Gate, Barn, Oak Tree, Growing Plot, Hay Bale, Water Trough, Doghouse, Tennis
  Ball, Rope Toy, and Bone.
- Every other visible ground/decor row is a buy-once permanent unlock. Placement,
  fence length, moving, copying, and recoloring do not cost additional tickets.
- Pet Memorial is generated by pet outcomes, hidden from the store, and never
  purchasable.
- The first named Corgi remains the one-time free onboarding pet with 20 Dog
  Food. Every later pet is an individual 1,200-ticket adoption and includes five
  servings of that species' food.
- Food is a repeat-purchase consumable at the prices defined by the pet-care
  catalog. Crop seeds remain unavailable for ticket purchase in v1.
- The Farm does not mint tickets and produce cannot be sold for tickets.

## Canonical architecture

Permanent Farm items register as shop `farm` in the shared ticket-shop registry.
The authenticated browser sends only an item id. The server catalog in
`platform-api/src/services/farm-ticket-catalog.mts` owns prices, and the shared
wallet transaction grants a durable `game_entitlements` row under game slug
`farm`.

Adoption and supplies deliberately do not use permanent entitlements. They use
trusted Farm actions:

- `POST /games/farm/adoptions`
- `POST /games/farm/supplies/purchases`

Both operations use the shared ticket debit primitive, an idempotency key, a
locked Farm loadout, a locked wallet, one database transaction, and server-owned
prices. Adoption creates the individual profile server-side, checks the pet cap
and water habitat, and grants five food. Supply purchase increments only the
requested trusted stack.

The generic Farm loadout save is not an economy operation. It validates paid
ground/decor against entitlements, refuses new pet instances after onboarding,
preserves server-created pet identity fields, and never permits supply counts to
increase. It still permits normal care progression, feeding, renaming, release,
crop progress, and editor changes to owned content.

Migration `053-farm-ticket-entitlements.sql` preserves non-starter ground and
decor already saved while the development catalog was free.

## Price source of truth

Exact permanent-item prices live only in
`platform-api/src/services/farm-ticket-catalog.mts`. Adoption and food prices
live in `platform-api/src/services/farm-economy-catalog.mts`. Browser catalogs
own presentation and placement metadata, not economic truth.
